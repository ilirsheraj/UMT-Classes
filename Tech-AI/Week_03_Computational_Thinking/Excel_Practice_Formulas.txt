EXCEL PRACTICE - COMPUTATIONAL THINKING IN A SPREADSHEET
Answer key: formulas and expected results
=====================================================================

Results are for the data as given in the workbook, before the
"change a number" tasks. "Copy down" means: write the formula in the
first cell, then drag the fill handle down. Excel adjusts the row
numbers by itself (the "loop").

SHEET 1 - CAKE COST   (multiplication, copy down, SUM, IF)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

        E12:  =B12*D12          -> 60   (Flour)

        E14:  =B14*D14          -> 80   (Eggs)
        E15:  =B15*D15          -> 225  (Butter)
        E16:  =B16*D16          -> 120  (Cocoa)

        E17:  =SUM(E12:E16)     -> 527

Task 3  Cost per slice
        E18:  =E17/H12          -> 52.7

        E19:  =IF(E17<=H13,"Within budget","Over budget")

        result "Within budget".

SHEET 4 - SHOP   (XLOOKUP, IF)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Task 1  Product name (copy down to B17)
        B13:  =XLOOKUP(A13,$G$13:$G$22,$H$13:$H$22,"Not found")

Task 2  Price and line total (copy down to C17 and E17)
        C13:  =XLOOKUP(A13,$G$13:$G$22,$J$13:$J$22,0)
        E13:  =C13*D13

        Expected results:
        Code  Product                   Price  Qty  Line total
        C01   Chocolate cake (whole)    2,400   1     2,400
        C03   Muffin                      250   6     1,500
        C07   Cappuccino                  200   4       800
        C10   Gift box                    350   1       350
        C09   Birthday candles (pack)     150   2       300

Task 3  Subtotal, delivery and total
        E18:  =SUM(E13:E17)        -> 5,350
        E19:  =IF(E18>=K13,0,K14)  -> 0   (free delivery)
        E20:  =E18+E19             -> 5,350

Task 4  Muffins 6 -> 2: subtotal 4,350 is below 5,000, so delivery
        is 300 and the total is 4,650.

Bonus   Code C99: with the 4th argument the product shows "Not found"
        and the price 0. Without the 4th argument Excel shows #N/A.

For older Excel without XLOOKUP (Excel 2019 or earlier):
        B13:  =IFERROR(INDEX($H$13:$H$22,MATCH(A13,$G$13:$G$22,0)),"Not found")
        C13:  =IFERROR(INDEX($J$13:$J$22,MATCH(A13,$G$13:$G$22,0)),0)

SHEET 5 - LOAN   (PMT, IF, COUNTIF)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Inputs: B14 loan 1,000,000 ALL, B15 rate 6%, B16 years 3,
        B17 income 70,000 ALL, B18 max share 40%

Task 1  B20  Monthly interest rate:  =B15/12          -> 0.50%
        B21  Number of payments:     =B16*12          -> 36

Task 2  B22  Monthly payment:        =PMT(B20,B21,-B14)
                                                      -> 30,422 ALL

Task 3  B23  Total paid:             =B22*B21         -> 1,095,190 ALL
        B24  Total interest:         =B23-B14         -> 95,190 ALL
        B25  Max payment allowed:    =B17*B18         -> 28,000 ALL

Task 4  B26  Decision:
             =IF(B22<=B25,"Approve","Offer a longer term")
                                      -> Offer a longer term

Task 5  Compare terms (copy down from row 14 to row 18)
        E14:  =PMT($B$15/12,D14*12,-$B$14)
        F14:  =E14*D14*12-$B$14
        G14:  =IF(E14<=$B$25,"Approve","Too high")

        Expected results:
        Years   Monthly payment   Total interest   Decision
          1         86,066            32,797       Too high
          2         44,321            63,695       Too high
          3         30,422            95,190       Too high
          4         23,485           127,281       Approve
          5         19,333           159,968       Approve

        Terms approved
        G20:  =COUNTIF(G14:G18,"Approve")       -> 2

Task 6  Income 90,000: max payment 36,000. The 3-year payment of
        30,422 is now approved. In the comparison, 3, 4 and 5 years
        are approved, so COUNTIF gives 3.

Why the minus sign in PMT? Excel follows money from your point of
view: the loan you receive is money in (+), the payments are money
out (-). Writing -B14 makes the payment come out as a positive number.
